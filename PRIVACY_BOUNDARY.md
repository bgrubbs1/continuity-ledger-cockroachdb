# Continuity Ledger privacy boundary

This contest project must use deliberately synthetic data only. Fictional incident records may describe invented services, tenants, agents, task states, embeddings, and recovery events. They must not reproduce or derive from Bradley's employer, customers, coworkers, household, precise location, private network, credentials, billing records, email, browsing activity, or other nonpublic information.

Secrets and account identifiers stay in ignored local environment files or the authorized cloud secret store. They must not appear in Git, screenshots, videos, logs, prompts, Operations Center records, or organizer-facing text.

No deployment, persistence, failover, recovery, latency, scale, availability, or security result may be claimed until the exact behavior has been executed and preserved as reproducible evidence.
